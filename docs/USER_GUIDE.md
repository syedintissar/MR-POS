# User Guide: Mr Printer & Tuner POS

> **Status: OUTLINE (Phase 1).** Each section is written when its module is built. The phase that fills it is shown in brackets. The guide is written for shop staff, uses plain language and screenshots, and has one section per daily task.

## 1. Getting started [Phase 2]
- Opening the system from any shop computer (`http://192.168.1.10`)
- Logging in, changing your password, logging out
- The screen layout: sidebar, top bar, notifications
- What you can see depends on your role (Admin, Manager, Cashier, Technician)

## 2. Selling at the counter (POS) [Phase 5]
- Scanning or searching products
- Changing quantity, removing a line
- Discounts (and when a manager must approve)
- Choosing a customer / walk-in
- Taking payment: cash, card, bank, Easypaisa/JazzCash, split payment
- Credit and partial payment
- Holding and resuming a sale
- Printing the receipt or A4 invoice; reprinting
- Voiding a sale (managers)
- Keyboard shortcuts cheat-sheet

## 3. Customers & collecting payments [Phase 6]
- Adding a customer, credit limits
- Receiving a payment against invoices
- Printing a customer statement
- Who owes money (outstanding and aging reports)

## 4. Repair, service & refill jobs [Phase 7]
- Receiving a printer/toner/cartridge (job card and slip)
- Assigning a technician
- Technician workflow: diagnosis, approval, parts, status updates
- Taking an advance
- Delivering the job and collecting payment
- Cancelling a job

## 5. Products & stock [Phase 3]
- Adding and editing products, prices, barcodes
- Printing barcode labels
- Stock adjustments and stock counts
- Low-stock list

## 6. Purchases & suppliers [Phase 4]
- Entering a supplier bill and receiving stock
- Paying suppliers
- Returning goods to a supplier

## 7. Returns [Phase 8]
- Customer returns: refund, store credit, exchange
- Damaged and defective items

## 8. Expenses [Phase 8]
- Recording an expense with a receipt photo

## 9. Reports & dashboard [Phase 8]
- Dashboard filters
- Daily closing
- Sales, profit, stock, customer and supplier reports
- Printing and exporting (PDF/Excel)

## 10. Administration [Phases 2 & 9]
- Users and roles
- Shop settings, invoice settings, payment methods
- **Backups**: checking backup health, Backup Now, USB rotation, downloading
- **Restore**: when and how (admins only)
- Audit log

## 11. Daily / weekly checklists [Phase 10]
- Opening: check the dashboard banner for backup warnings
- Closing: daily closing report, cash count
- Weekly: swap the USB backup drive, review low stock and aging
